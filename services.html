Services

Complete care, under one roof

Family, cosmetic, restorative and orthodontic dentistry, implants and sedation, with direct billing to most insurance plans and financing options available.

01

Same Day Smile Makeovers

Advanced techniques, including our in-office CEREC machine, to refresh your smile with precision in just one visit. Complimentary consultation.

02

Dental Implants

A titanium post that heals into the jaw and supports a crown, replacing one tooth, several, or a full arch, and securing loose dentures. Complimentary consultation.

03

Family Dentistry

Care for every age, from toddlers to grandparents: preventive check-ups, cleanings and oral exams every 3 to 6 months.

04

Cosmetic Dentistry

A personalized plan for brighter, more even teeth, built around your goals and budget.

05

Teeth Whitening

In-office and take-home professional whitening to lift stains and discoloration.

06

Dental Bonding

Tooth-coloured resin shaped and hardened in place to fix chipped, misshapen or uneven teeth.

07

Porcelain Veneers

Thin porcelain shells fitted over front teeth to cover nearly any cosmetic imperfection.

08

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, bridges and implants to repair chipped teeth and replace missing ones. Crowns and bridges can be made in our in-house lab.

09

Dental Sedation

Nitrous oxide (laughing gas), oral and IV sedation for anxious patients or extensive treatment, with monitoring throughout.

10

Wisdom Teeth Extractions

Removal of problem third molars that cause pain, infection or crowding, plus standard extractions, under sedation.

11

Orthodontics: Braces & Invisalign

Traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, or virtually invisible Invisalign aligners to straighten teeth and correct bites.

12

Prompt Emergency Care

Walk-ins and emergencies welcome, 7 days a week.
