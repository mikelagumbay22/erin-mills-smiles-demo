Meet the lead dentist

Dr. Marwan Hameed

With more than 20 years in dentistry, Dr. Hameed focuses on cosmetic veneers, smile makeovers, sleep-sedation dentistry, implants and full-mouth rehabilitation. He has placed thousands of implants and teaches implant courses to other dentists.

  • B.D.S (Iraq) · D.M.D (Canada, UBC 2011)
  • Diplomate, International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI)
  • Member, American Academy of Oral Implantologists
